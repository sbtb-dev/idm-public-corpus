Gaming advertising's growth story is native format supply expanding into gaming audiences. It is not a channel forming. Every format inside the reported growth already belongs to display, video, or sponsorship, and each one books to that channel's line. More formats in the bundle is not a bundle becoming a channel.

July 23 supplied the demonstration. Microsoft opened a new route into gaming audiences, priced it, and disclosed exactly what it does not touch.

## Microsoft priced access in ad time
Microsoft began an Xbox Insider test of ad-supported cloud streaming. A player watches a two-minute block of ads, then streams for one hour. After the hour, another block buys another hour. Microsoft states the ads run before the session and do not interrupt gameplay.

That unit is video. Its output is a completion. A completion earned in front of a game session is the same evidentiary object as a completion earned in front of anything else — the incentive raises the completion rate and changes nothing about what a completion is. The IAB's own gaming framework routes gaming video to video and measures it against video's accredited standards. The routing was settled before I said a word about it.

So the spend clears as video. It books as video. No gaming line is created by it, and none is needed.


## The catalog is the finding
The test's free-to-play titles include Call of Duty: Warzone, Genshin Impact, and War Thunder. Those are live-service environments, and they run the most complete consumer response instrumentation in commercial media — activation, engagement, retention, and monetization conversion, observed directly, inside one environment, because the business model cannot operate otherwise.

An advertiser in this test receives none of it. The buy is the door. The operator keeps the room. Microsoft's own disclosure states the position exactly: the game itself remains unchanged. The environment behind the ad is capable of producing direct behavioral evidence of consumer response at pre-conversion funnel stages. The unit sold in front of it produces a completion.

That is the advertising boundary, drawn by the seller rather than by me.

## The price tells you the evidence
The unit is denominated in minutes of access. Two minutes of advertising buys sixty minutes of play. Anything priced in duration of access is priced on exposure, whatever sits behind the door.

A media planner can run that as a field test. If the rate card counts time, the report will count impressions and completions. Nothing in the transaction observes what the consumer did in response to the brand, because nothing in the transaction was built to.

## Why this supply scales and the channel does not
Preroll into a cloud session scales because it is compatible with infrastructure that already exists. It clears through video exchanges, reports against video standards, and requires no new buying motion, no new measurement, and no new budget line. Compatibility with the video stack is the reason it grows.

Immersive media inventory has none of that. It produces evidence no established channel produces, which is precisely why no established channel's infrastructure can absorb it. In other words, the formats that scale fastest in gaming scale because they are gaming versions of recognized formats. They are native formats, pointed at a gaming audience, running on the rails they were always going to run on.

That is the growth story read correctly. Video grew into a gaming audience. The growth is real and it is large, and it confirms the count rather than disturbing it: gaming media budgets do not exist, because the spend they would contain is already filed under the channel that delivered it.

## A forecast, marked as one
Amazon did something adjacent on the same day. It put a Games tab inside Prime Video on Fire TV, where Prime members start Luna titles from the same app they use for video and sports. No advertising was announced, and I am not going to claim one was.

Here's where I'll claim it lands. The placement is distribution into a video product with a video ad stack behind it. When/if Amazon monetizes that shelf, the inventory will be sold, served, and reported through Prime Video's advertising infrastructure, on video's terms.

That is a prediction, and it can be checked. If the revenue is ever reported against a gaming line, measured to gaming standards, or bought on a gaming motion, I am wrong and the count is wrong with me. 

## What is still missing
One gaming-related spend has no native home and no line in any book. It is the advertising that produces direct behavioral evidence of consumer response at pre-conversion funnel stages, captured by the environment's native instrumentation, attributable to the brand's presence. Display cannot produce it. Video cannot produce it. Sponsorship cannot produce it.

July 23 added supply to the bundle and nothing to that line.

The bundle grew. The channel is still unbuilt.
